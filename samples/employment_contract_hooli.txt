EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
(Synthetic sample document for demonstration purposes only)

This Employment Agreement is entered into on January 10, 2025 between Hooli Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated in India ("Employer"), and Priya Nair ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
1.1 The Employee is employed as Senior Software Engineer and shall report to the Director of Engineering.
1.2 The Employee shall devote full working time to the Employer and shall not engage in any other employment without prior written consent.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 The Employer shall pay an annual base salary of INR 2,400,000, payable monthly in arrears, less statutory deductions.
2.2 The Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of base salary, at the Employer's discretion.
2.3 The Employee shall receive stock options over 4,000 equity shares, vesting over four (4) years with a one (1) year cliff.

3. LEAVE
3.1 The Employee is entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave and 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
3.2 Unused annual leave up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next year.

4. PROBATION AND TERMINATION
4.1 The first six (6) months of employment are a probationary period during which either party may terminate on fifteen (15) days' written notice.
4.2 After probation, either party may terminate by giving sixty (60) days' written notice, or the Employer may pay salary in lieu of notice.
4.3 The Employer may terminate without notice for gross misconduct, fraud or material breach of this Agreement.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INVENTIONS
5.1 The Employee shall keep confidential all proprietary information of the Employer during and after employment.
5.2 All inventions, code and work product created by the Employee in the course of employment are owned exclusively by the Employer, and the Employee assigns all rights to the Employer.

6. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
6.1 For twelve (12) months after termination, the Employee shall not work for a direct competitor of the Employer in India.
6.2 For eighteen (18) months after termination, the Employee shall not solicit any employee or customer of the Employer.

7. GOVERNING LAW
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in Bengaluru under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before a sole arbitrator.
